The three technique fixes that unlock more reps
1
Breathe on a rhythm, not when you remember. Exhale loudly on every single throw — make it audible. Inhale as you descend into the squat. Most people hold their breath at rep 5–6 and that's exactly when the set collapses. The breathing rhythm IS the movement rhythm.
2
Catch the ball at forehead height, elbows in. If the ball drifts low or your elbows flare out on the catch, you're absorbing the weight instead of using it to load the squat. The ball should land and immediately pull you down into the next rep — not stop you.
3
No pause at the bottom or top. The squat is a rebound, not a sit. The moment you reach the bottom, drive up. The moment the ball leaves your hands at the top, start descending. Any pause breaks momentum and makes the next throw feel twice as heavy.
Race day wall ball mindset
