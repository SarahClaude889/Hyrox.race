About

I've spent 9+ years designing leadership, manager capability and organisational learning across APAC and EMEA — from global banks and pharma companies to a vulnerable-youth organisation in Botswana. I'm now based in the UK.

I partner with senior leaders and People teams to translate business priorities into practical learning ecosystems: blended programmes, experiential journeys and capability interventions that get measured in behaviour change — at 30, 60 and 90 days — not attendance.

My practice brings together facilitation, behavioural science and psychometrics, AI-enabled learning, and mental-health-informed design. The strategy has to hold up, and the room has to feel safe enough for something real to happen in it.

Process

How a programme comes together

The same four moves, whether it's a single workshop or a multi-cohort rollout.

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, performance data, and a gap analysis to find out what's actually breaking — I've run 20+ structured executive interviews on a single engagement.

02

Design

Learning objectives, session architecture, and materials built around how adults actually learn and retain skills.

03

Deliver

Facilitation in the room or on the call — practice, live feedback, and real scenarios instead of lecture time.

04

Echo

Follow-up on what actually changed — behaviour, confidence, results at 30, 60 and 90 days — fed straight back into the next iteration.
