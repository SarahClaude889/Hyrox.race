01 · My story & my why

Where the ripple began

For 9+ years across APAC and EMEA — and now from the UK — I've partnered with senior leaders and People teams as a learning and leadership consultant and facilitator, turning business priorities into learning that gets measured in behaviour change, not attendance.

The ripple began early. At 19, travelling alone through Morocco, I met a young woman in a textile workshop. We had no shared language — so we spoke in gestures, expressions and curiosity. Before leaving, I gave her a Hong Kong two-dollar coin; its bauhinia flower made her smile for the first time. In return, she wrapped my scarf around my head in the Moroccan style. Two strangers from very different worlds, meeting without many words.

I did not change her life, and I never learned her story. But that small exchange changed something in the space between us — and it stayed with me.

Years later, I recognised the same pattern in learning and facilitation: change does not always begin with a grand gesture. Sometimes it begins when one person feels heard and sees a new possibility.

That is why I do this work: to create the conditions for small human shifts that can continue rippling beyond the room.

Read the whole story

In that small rural town, I visited the textile workshop and noticed her working quietly in the room. She seemed reserved, perhaps a little tired. Something in me wanted to reach across the distance.

We may never know where a moment like that travels next. A question, a gesture or a seed planted today may influence another conversation, relationship or decision tomorrow. Meaningful change does not always need a grand intervention — sometimes it needs someone to feel noticed, to encounter a different perspective, or to receive a small invitation to respond in a new way.
