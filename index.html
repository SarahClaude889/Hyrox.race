About

I've spent the last 9+ years working in the gap between the strategy deck and Monday morning — the place where learning programs either take root or quietly get forgotten.

As a learning consultant and facilitator, I design and deliver programs for teams that need more than a slide deck: they need practice, honest feedback, and a real reason to work differently once the session ends.

My work spans instructional design, live facilitation, and train-the-trainer enablement — always built around what a room full of adults will actually use, not what looks tidiest on a curriculum map.

Process

How a program comes together

The same four moves, whether it's a single workshop or a multi-cohort rollout.

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, performance data, and a gap analysis to find out what's actually breaking — not just what's been asked for.

02

Design

Learning objectives, session architecture, and materials built around how adults actually learn and retain skills.

03

Deliver

Facilitation in the room or on the call — practice, live feedback, and real scenarios instead of lecture time.

04

Measure

Follow-up data on behavior change and business impact, fed straight back into the next iteration of the program.
