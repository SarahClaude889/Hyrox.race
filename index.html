About

I've spent the last 9+ years working in the moment right after a workshop ends — when a shift in thinking either ripples outward into someone's team, family, and choices, or quietly fades by Monday.

As a learning and talent development consultant and facilitator, I bring together instructional design, group facilitation, and psychological training — emotional intelligence, behavioural styles, internal family systems — to help people not just learn something, but change how they see, decide, and show up.

My work sits where strategy meets the nervous system: the design has to hold up, and the room has to feel safe enough for something real to happen in it.

Process

How a program comes together

The same four moves, whether it's a single workshop or a multi-cohort rollout.

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, performance data, and a gap analysis to find out what's actually breaking — not just what's been asked for.

02

Design

Learning objectives, session architecture, and materials built around how adults actually learn and retain skills.

03

Deliver

Facilitation in the room or on the call — practice, live feedback, and real scenarios instead of lecture time.

04

Echo

Follow-up on what actually changed — behaviour, confidence, results — fed straight back into the next iteration.
